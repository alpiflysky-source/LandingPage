How it works

From the mountain hut's request to delivery at altitude: the method, the flight software and how it decides whether to fly.

From request
to delivery

Three steps, from request to delivery. The flying is always done by an authorised drone operator.

  1. Request and planning

    The mountain hut chooses the day, the weight in kg and the time slotdays ahead

    The software replies whether a flight is possible, and whenimmediately

  2. Mission optimisation

    Nearby requests become a single missionthe day before

    Route flyable and landing pad clear: take-off5 min before

  3. Delivery at destination

    A signal alerts anyone nearby2 min before arrival

    If someone enters the area, the descent stopsduring descent

How the software
decides

Hour by hour, it compares the weather along the route with the drone's limits and with flights already flown. Then it indicates whether to go, and when.

Flyable
Within limits, in conditions already proven.
Marginal
Close to the limit or not yet tested: the drone operator decides.
Not flyable
Beyond the limit. No take-off.
