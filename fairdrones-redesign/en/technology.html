The technology

The software, the station network with the Fairbox and the wind research.

The software

  • Collects requests

    Different customers order from the same app.

  • Assesses the flight

    Weather, route and payload checked against the drone's limits.

  • Organises missions

    Nearby requests become a single flight.

One network per valley,
not a mast for every hut

The heart of FairDrones: one reference station per valley and a node on the landing pads where needed. For drones of any make.

  • Valley station. One per valley: the weather and radio reference for all routes.
  • Landing pad node, the Fairbox. Where needed: checks that the delivery area is clear and safe.
  • Mountain hut app. Confirms that the area is clear and tracks the delivery.
  • Drone-agnostic: the same data serves every drone operator and every aircraft.

The Fairbox, eyes on the landing pad

A solar-powered node where the drone lands: it measures wind and visibility and checks that the area is clear.

It decides on its own, for its own location:

Delivery OKWaitCancel

The status reaches the pilot via the server: the Fairbox never controls the drone.
